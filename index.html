01 / About

About me

Before coming to Japan, I finished my M.Sc. in Water and Environmental Management at DUET, Bangladesh, first in my class. For my thesis I used river models to show how floods in the Matamuhuri River may grow in the future.

Right now I am building a computer model of the Sangu River in Bangladesh. It turns rainfall into river flow and flood maps, so I can test how forests and wetlands could protect nearby villages.
