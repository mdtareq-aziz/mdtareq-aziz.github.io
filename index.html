01 / About

About me

Before Hokkaido, I completed an M.Sc. in Water and Environmental Management at DUET, Bangladesh, finishing first in my department, with a thesis on future flood hazard in the Matamuhuri River basin using HEC-HMS and HEC-RAS. As a research assistant I worked on future flood vulnerability under climate and land-use change in the Dhaka River basin.

My current work builds a SWAT+ model of the Sangu River basin and couples it with HEC-RAS to simulate flooding under CMIP6 climate and land-use scenarios, then tests nature-based solutions inside those models.
